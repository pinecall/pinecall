$ pinecall walks --help
Usage: pinecall walks [options] [command]

the walks a tutor agent published, recorded in its voice, which a visitor plays
with no call

Options:
  -h, --help             display help for command

Commands:
  list [options]         each walk: its sentences, its length, the voice it was
                         recorded in
  play [options] <walk>  one walk played here, a sentence at a time
  help [command]         display help for command

A tutor agent (one that extends Tutor from @pinecall/dot/agent) records each macro of its folder in
its own voice as `pinecall start` or a deployed app holds it, and publishes them as walks: a visitor
plays one from the page with no call. These read what it published in this env (--prod for
production's): list says each walk's sentences, length and voice; play plays one here, a sentence at
a time, on the first player this machine has.


$ pinecall walks list --help
Usage: pinecall walks list [options]

each walk: its sentences, its length, the voice it was recorded in

Options:
  --agent <slug>
  -h, --help      display help for command

$ pinecall walks play --help
Usage: pinecall walks play [options] <walk>

one walk played here, a sentence at a time

Arguments:
  walk            its name or its title

Options:
  --agent <slug>
  -h, --help      display help for command
