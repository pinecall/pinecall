$ pinecall lexicon --help
Usage: pinecall lexicon [options] [command]

an agent's words: how the voice says them and what the ears must know

Options:
  -h, --help                 display help for command

Commands:
  show [options]             the words and how they are said and heard, in the
                             three corners
  add [options] <word>       how a word is spoken
  hear [options] <words...>  the words the ears must know
  rm [options] <words...>    words taken out of both
  history [options]          every version of a corner's lexicon, who set it and
                             why
  help [command]             display help for command

The lexicon is the agent's says and hears, which the class never sets. add says how a word is
spoken; hear names the words the ears must know; rm takes words out of both. Whole and
versioned like the settings: the corner is yours (--team: the org's own).

Examples
  $ pinecall lexicon add DKV --say "de ka uve"
  $ pinecall lexicon hear "Vidal Ferrán"
  $ pinecall lexicon history
