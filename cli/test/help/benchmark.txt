$ pinecall benchmark --help
Usage: pinecall benchmark [options] [comparison...]

the report of comparisons already played: each setup against two bars, every
call with its recording and its log

Arguments:
  comparison                      the comparisons, as pinecall simulate named
                                  them (cmp_…): several are one table

Options:
  --condition <name:comparisons>  the comparisons played under one condition, as
                                  television-20:cmp_…,cmp_…; again for each, the
                                  first being the line the others are read
                                  against
  --out <dir>                     the folder the report is written into
                                  (default: "benchmark")
  --floor <share>                 the share of counted calls a setup must
                                  complete, 0 to 1 (0.9)
  --ceiling <seconds>             the middle reply a setup may take, in seconds
                                  (1.5)
  --baseline <report.json>        an earlier report: each setup is held against
                                  its own row there
  --gate                          exit 1 when no setup clears both bars, or one
                                  fell against the baseline
  --counted-only                  a report to publish: the calls that did not
                                  count are left out, listed nowhere and their
                                  recordings not kept
  --gate-under <condition>        exit 1 unless one setup clears both bars under
                                  this condition too, beside the first one's
                                  line; again for another; turns --gate on
  --price <model=usd>             a model the gateway priced nothing for, priced
                                  in the report at these dollars a minute of its
                                  session (grok-voice-3.0=0.08); again for
                                  another
  -h, --help                      display help for command

The report of comparisons `pinecall simulate` already played, written as a folder to open or
publish: index.html (the page), report.json (every number of it), report.data.js (the same, for
the page opened from the disk) and calls/ (each call's recording, as the gateway kept it). Several
comparisons are one table: a caller rung again under the same setup is another round of it.

Each setup is held to two bars: --floor, the share of its counted calls in which the caller got
what it came for, and --ceiling, the middle of its calls' replies in seconds. With several setups
the one to take is the cheapest per completed call of those that clear both; with one, the agent
passes or fails each bar. A share comes with the range it plausibly sits in after that many calls
(95%), and each round's count beside it shows how far one run moves from the next. A call its
vendor refused, the platform lost or whose own caller broke is listed and counted in nothing.

--gate exits 1 when no setup clears both bars, and 2 when nobody judged a call. --baseline holds
each setup against its row in an earlier report.json: a setup regressed when its share fell by more
than that report's own rounds moved among themselves, and with --gate that fails too. --json prints
report.json. `pinecall simulate … --report` places the calls and writes the report in one go.

--condition names the comparisons played on one line, again for each: the same callers under the
same setups on a clean line, with a television behind them, on a bad network. The first is the line
every other is read against, and the table and the verdict are its own; the report adds a column a
condition, each setup's share there and what the line moved most, and the verdict says which setups
clear both bars under every one. --gate-under names a condition one setup must clear both bars
under too, beside the first one's line, and turns --gate on.

Examples
  $ pinecall benchmark cmp_4f1c2a9be07d
  $ pinecall benchmark cmp_4f1c2a9be07d cmp_91aa03c7d2e1 --out reports/november
  $ pinecall benchmark cmp_4f1c2a9be07d --floor 0.95 --ceiling 1.2 --gate
  $ pinecall benchmark cmp_4f1c2a9be07d --baseline reports/october/report.json --gate
  $ pinecall benchmark --condition clean:cmp_4f1c2a9be07d --condition television-20:cmp_91aa03c7d2e1 --gate-under television-20
