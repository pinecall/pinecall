$ pinecall personas --help
Usage: pinecall personas [options] [command]

an agent's synthetic callers: Pinecall's switched on or off, its own written,
generated, tried

Options:
  -h, --help              display help for command

Commands:
  list [options]
  show [options] <name>
  add [options] <name>    write one: --goal and --style are needed
  edit [options] <name>   change what is named and leave the rest
  rm [options] <name>     the caller dropped; one of Pinecall's is switched off
                          instead
  on [options] <name>     one of Pinecall's switched on for the agent
  off [options] <name>    one of Pinecall's switched off for the agent
  try [options] <name>    one call against the agent: simulate, without the
                          judge
  generate [options] [n]  callers written by the judge model from the agent;
                          printed, and kept with --save
  push [options]          the personas still in files, sent to the gateway once
  help [command]          display help for command

A persona is a caller a model plays: a goal, a style, the facts they may state about themselves,
what they expect of the agent — and, on a spoken line, how they act. There is no script. Pinecall's
library comes first — realism and a red team — each off until switched on; then the agent's own.

add needs --goal and --style; edit changes what is named and leaves the rest. generate writes N
callers (3 unless said) from the agent's prompt, tools and knowledge — printed, and kept with
--save; one eval each, none on your own judge key. push sends the personas still in files once.

Examples
  $ pinecall personas add price-shopper --goal "get a price for a deep clean" --style "blunt, impatient" --fact "their name=Tom Baker"

write one: --goal and --style are needed

  --agent <name>
  --file <agent.tsx>
  --goal <goal>            what they want
  --style <style>          how they talk
  --about <about>          who they are, in a line
